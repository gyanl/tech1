### Brief

In teams of up to 3, take a product idea from concept to a **working, deployed web application** that a stranger could use — built primarily by directing AI tools — and present it at Demo Day.

### The shape of it

- **Kickoff (Week 10):** form teams, pitch topics, submit a one-page proposal (problem, users, core flow, stack sketch)
- **Sprint 1 (Week 11):** core flow working end-to-end, however ugly. Live crit.
- **Sprint 2 (Week 12):** ship-ready — polish, empty/error states, performance and security pass. Feature freeze at end of week.
- **Demo Day (Week 13):** 7-minute live demo + process story, 3-minute Q&A.

### Requirements

1. **Deployed and public** — a URL, working on mobile, on the day of the demo
2. **Has a back-end** — stores or fetches real data (database and/or external API)
3. **Considered as a product** — onboarding, empty states, error states, visual polish
4. **Process documentation** — your proposal, sprint plans, prompt logs, dead ends, and what you'd do differently
5. **You understand your stack** — every team member can answer "where does this data live?" and "what happens when I click this?"

An AI feature is encouraged if it earns its place, not required.

### Scope advice

The best projects are one small thing that fully works. "Splitwise for hostels" beats "a social network." You will cut scope in Week 11 — teams that pick a small idea get to spend Week 12 on polish instead of triage.

### Evaluation

| Criteria                  | Marks  | Description                                                                  |
| ------------------------- | ------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Timeliness                | 2      | Proposal and sprint milestones hit on time.                                  |
| Concept                   | 3      | Clarity of problem, fit of solution, sensible scope.                         |
| Working Product           | 5      | Deployed, functional core flow; handles errors; works on mobile.             |
| Design & Polish           | 4      | Visual quality, states, coherence — the design work AI skips.                |
| Stack Understanding       | 3      | Team can explain how their own product works, at this course's depth.        |
| Presentation & Process    | 3      | Demo quality and honesty/completeness of process documentation.              |
| **Total**                 | **20** |                                                                              |
