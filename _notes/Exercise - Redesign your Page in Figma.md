### Design the page you just built

You have a live website, but it looks like the template. Before we learn CSS next week, decide **what you actually want it to look like** — in the tool you already know.

### Steps

1. **Get your page into Figma.** Use the [html.to.design](https://www.figma.com/community/plugin/1159123024924461424) plugin (or take a full-page screenshot and drop it in) so you're working from your real page, not a blank canvas.
2. **Change the words first.** Real copy, your name, your actual projects. Design decisions are easier when the content is true.
3. **Change the type.** Pick a typeface and set a hierarchy — heading, subheading, body. Notice how much of the page's character this alone decides.
4. **Change the colours.** Background, text, links. Keep it to two or three; check it's still readable.
5. **Then push further, if you want.** Add sections, a projects grid, an about page, images.

### Things to keep in mind

- Design for a **browser**, not a poster: it will be viewed at many widths, and text reflows. Sketch a phone-width version too.
- Note anything you're unsure how to build. Bring those questions — next week is exactly about turning this into CSS.
- Keep your Figma file. In [[Lecture 2]] you'll rebuild your live page to match it, and we'll compare the two.
