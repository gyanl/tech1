Similar courses at other universities, and places to go deeper.

#### [Vibe Coding (T564A)](https://kbrennan.scholars.harvard.edu/sites/g/files/omnuum5186/files/2026-02/T564A_2025_Syllabus.pdf)

Harvard Graduate School of Education — Karen Brennan. Creative building with AI code generation.

#### [CS50's Understanding Technology](https://cs50.harvard.edu/technology/)

Harvard — technology fundamentals "for people who don't (yet) consider themselves computer persons." Free, full materials.

#### [CS50's Web Programming with Python and JavaScript](https://cs50.harvard.edu/web/)

Harvard — a deeper, code-first web course. Good to raid for individual topic explanations.

#### [Mobile Web Design & Development (67-240)](https://www.cmu.edu/information-systems/images/syllabi/67240-mobile-web-design-dev-f17.pdf)

Carnegie Mellon — user-centered, project-assessed web/mobile course.

#### [Web Design Fundamentals](https://courses.newschool.edu/courses/NCOM3210/)

The New School — a design-school framing of web fundamentals.

#### Industry courses on the AI-for-designers stack
