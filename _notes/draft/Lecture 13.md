## Demo Day

Final presentations for [[Project - Final Project]].

#### Format
- Each team: **7 minutes** — live demo of the deployed product (no slides for the demo itself), followed by the story: problem, process, what broke, what you learned
- **3 minutes** Q&A from the class and guests
- Audience votes on superlatives: Most Useful, Most Polished, Best Save (biggest recovery from disaster)

#### What I'm looking for
- A working, deployed thing a stranger could use
- Evidence you understood your stack — you can answer "where does this data live?" and "what happens when I click this?"
- Honest process documentation, including AI prompts/logs and dead ends

#### After demos
- Course retrospective: what the map of the stack looks like now vs week 1
- Where to go from here — resources in [[Courses]]
