### iOS vs Android, from a product team's chair

Compare and contrast the iOS and Android ecosystems for app development and distribution, highlighting the differences that would actually change a product decision.

### Steps

1. **Pick a lens: a concrete app.** Imagine your team is shipping a specific product (a food delivery app, a meditation app, your final project). Compare the ecosystems *for that product*, not in the abstract.
2. **Research the differences across:**
	- **Design** — Human Interface Guidelines vs Material Design; what the same screen looks like on each
	- **Development** — native (Swift/Kotlin) vs cross-platform (React Native/Flutter) vs web/PWA; what each costs in fidelity and money
	- **Distribution** — App Store vs Play Store: review times, rules, fees, who gets rejected and why
	- **Audience** — market share where *your* users live; device diversity; who pays for apps
	- **Distribution without a store** — could this be a PWA on a URL instead? What would you lose?
3. **Make a recommendation.** For your imagined product: which platform first, or both, or PWA? One paragraph, defensible.
4. **Use AI as a research assistant,** but verify anything that sounds like a number. Store fees, review times and market share all change, and a model will cite a stale figure with total confidence — you saw why in [[Lecture 7 - AI Features]]. Cite your sources with dates.

### Submission

- A comparison one-pager (table encouraged) + your recommendation paragraph. 5-minute presentation in class.
