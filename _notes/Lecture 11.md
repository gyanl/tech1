## Studio 1 — Build Sprint

Full working session for [[Project - Final Project]].

#### Format
- **First 15 min** — standup: each team states what's built, what's next, what's blocked
- **Working time** — build with AI tools; I circulate for unblocking and prompting help
- **Last 30 min** — crit: each team demos their work-in-progress live (deployed URL, not slides)

#### Themes that come up in crits (and get mini-lectures on demand)
- Scope-cutting: the art of the smaller version that actually ships
- When the AI is stuck in a loop: strategies for getting unstuck (restart, reframe, read the error)
- Keeping your repo clean enough to not lose work

#### By end of week
- Sprint 1 goal met: core flow working end-to-end, however ugly
